Privacy & analytics

This site counts page views with its own simple analytics. It uses no cookies and no third parties.

What we collect

  • The page path you viewed (no query strings), the date, the website you came from (domain only) and a device type (desktop, mobile or tablet).
  • A count of unique visitors per day. This uses a one-way hash of your IP address and browser details that is deleted at the end of the day. Your IP address itself is never stored.

What we don't collect

Cookies, IP addresses, user agents, account or Steam/Discord IDs, location, or anything that follows you between days or sites. The data is not shared or sold.

Why and how long

To see which parts of the site are used (legitimate interest). Aggregate counts are deleted after 13 months. Only site admins can view them.

Opt out

We honour Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control automatically. You can also opt out here. Your choice is remembered with one small first-party preference cookie (dlns_optout, 1 year) plus a matching browser setting. It contains no identifier and is used only to remember that you opted out.
